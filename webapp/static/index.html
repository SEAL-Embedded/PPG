          Methodology
          
      
            SPI = +0.30·z(log RMSSD) + 0.20·z(log HFnu) − 0.25·z(log LF/HF)
            − 0.15·z(SampEn) + 0.10·z(SD1/SD2). Cohort z-norm reference is
            the ECG-derived feature distribution; the same z-stats are applied
            to PPG features so the two SPIs are on the same scale.
          
      
          
      
            Frequency-domain features use Lomb-Scargle periodogram integration
            over LF (0.04 – 0.15 Hz) and HF (0.15 – 0.40 Hz). Sample entropy:
            m=2, r=0.2·SDNN. Poincaré: closed-form Brennan 2001. Quality weight:
            σ(2·(SSQI − 0.5)) · σ(20·(0.05 − ZSQIµ)), clipped to [0.05, 1.0].
          
      
          
      
            Burgess 1997 · Boudreau 2013 · Cabiddu 2012 · Yi 2022 · Brennan 2001 ·
            Tulppo 1996 · Lomb 1976 · Schäfer & Vagedes 2013 · Richman & Moorman 2000 ·
            Hartmann/Charlton 2019 · Task Force ESC/NASPE 1996.
